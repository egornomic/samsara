# Samsara privacy statement

Samsara uses information from your open browser tabs to display a visual tab switcher. It does not operate a backend, include analytics or advertising, or send tab titles, page addresses, or preview screenshots to the developer.

## Information used on your device

Samsara reads open-tab titles, URLs, favicon URLs, and recent activation information to label and order the switcher. It captures visible tab images to display previews. Those images can contain anything visible on the captured page, including personal or sensitive information.

The overlay listens for keyboard modifier, selection, and cancellation events so you can operate the switcher. It does not record typed text or maintain a keystroke history.

## Storage and retention

Preview images and capture timestamps are stored locally in Chrome's session storage. They are not synchronized through a Samsara account or uploaded to a server. The extension removes a tab's cached preview when that tab closes. Session storage is cleared when the browser restarts or the extension is disabled, reloaded, updated, or removed.

Tab-switching state is held in memory. Keyboard shortcut assignments are managed by the browser.

## Network activity

When a screenshot is unavailable, the switcher may display a tab's favicon using the URL supplied by the browser. Loading that image can contact the website or its image provider, subject to the browser's cache and network behavior. Samsara has no analytics endpoint and does not send browsing data to the publisher.

## Your controls

You can change site access, disable the extension, or remove it through your browser's extension settings. Restricting site access can prevent previews or the overlay from working on those pages. Incognito access is controlled separately by the browser.

## Contact

Use the developer contact link on Samsara's Chrome Web Store listing for privacy questions. Avoid including sensitive tab contents or screenshots in a support request.
