# Store listing reference

## Name

samsara

## Summary

Preview and cycle through open tabs with user-configured keyboard shortcuts.

## Suggested category

Productivity

## Detailed description

Find your next tab without leaving the keyboard.

Samsara displays a visual switcher for the open tabs in your current window. Tabs are ordered by recent activity, making it easy to return to the page you were just using.

- Hold your shortcut's modifier key and repeat the shortcut to cycle through tabs.
- Release the modifier to switch, or press Escape to cancel.
- Recognize tabs by their titles, website addresses, and cached page previews.
- See your open-tab count on the toolbar icon.
- Configure your shortcuts through Chrome's extension keyboard settings.

Samsara processes tab information and stores preview images locally in your browser session. It has no account requirement, analytics, or advertising. Preview images can contain sensitive page content; see the privacy statement for storage details and favicon requests.

Chrome protects some pages from extension overlays. On those pages, Samsara switches tabs directly. Previews become available as you visit tabs and the browser allows captures.

Default shortcuts are Ctrl+Shift+Comma and Ctrl+Shift+Period on Windows and Linux, and Command+Comma and Command+Shift+Comma on macOS. If Chrome reports a conflict, assign your preferred shortcuts in the extension settings.
